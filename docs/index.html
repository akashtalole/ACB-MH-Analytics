ACB Maharashtra Analytics

Trap cases, disproportionate-assets cases and published FIRs / press releases of the Anti-Corruption Bureau, Maharashtra. (अँटी करप्शन ब्युरो, महाराष्ट्र राज्य)

Independent, unofficial project built from public data on acb.maharashtra.gov.in. A trap or FIR is an allegation, not a conviction.

Data notes

    • Sources: the official statistics PDF (yearly, region and department tables, transcribed from a Marathi scan and cross-checked against its printed totals) and the public FIR and press release index pages.
    • Dates: a handful of source dates are typos (e.g. year 0021, 2030). Obvious zero-padded years are repaired; others are left undated.
    • Case PDFs are scanned images. Extracting accused details, designations and trap amounts per case (OCR) is planned for a later phase.
    • Code and data: github.com/akashtalole/ACB-MH-Analytics. Refreshed daily by a scheduled workflow.
